# Clouvent Project Brief

This document defines what Clouvent is, who it serves, what it offers, and how the website should support the business. It works alongside `CLAUDE.md` (technical/design constitution) as the business source of truth for Phase 2 and beyond. Where information is genuinely missing, it is marked **[NEEDS INPUT]** rather than assumed.

## 1. Company Overview

Clouvent is a premium digital presence studio, Australia-first, focused initially on photographers and visual/creative businesses, with a long-term goal of international expansion. Core service model: **BUILD → MANAGE → GROW**.

**[NEEDS INPUT]** Legal entity name, ABN, base location within Australia, founding story.

## 2. Brand Positioning

Clouvent is explicitly **not** a cheap freelance web-dev service and **not** a DIY builder alternative. It is positioned around premium digital presence: design, performance, ongoing management, and growth — delivered as a continuous partnership rather than a one-off build.

*Positioning statement:* "Clouvent is a premium digital presence studio for photographers and visual/creative businesses — building, managing, and growing a web presence with the same standard of craft as the work it showcases."

## 3. Target Market

**Australia-first, with a focus on photographers and visual/creative businesses.**

This is the initial focus, not a permanent restriction — Clouvent is not limited to photographers long-term.

## 4. Ideal Customer Profile

Independent or small-studio Australian photographers and visual/creative businesses whose photography quality outpaces their web presence — typically running on outdated or template/DIY-builder sites, with little to no analytics/SEO discipline or ongoing digital management.

**[NEEDS INPUT]** Direct confirmation of this profile beyond the Once Upon a Time Photography pattern it's inferred from.

## 5. Customer Problems

- Outdated or generic template/DIY-builder websites that undersell the work
- No analytics or Search Console visibility
- No ongoing digital management or maintenance
- Inconsistent or absent paid social strategy
- Portfolio presentation that doesn't match the quality of the photography

## 6. Value Proposition

"A premium website build followed by real ongoing management and growth support — not a one-off freelance handoff. We design, develop, maintain, and grow your digital presence as a continuous partnership."

## 7. Services

Launch structure: a single Services page with three sections.

**BUILD**
- Website design
- Website development
- Website redesign
- Performance-focused implementation

**MANAGE**
- Website maintenance
- Content/website updates
- Analytics monitoring (Google Analytics)
- Google Search Console monitoring
- Ongoing digital support

**GROW**
- Paid social (Facebook/Instagram)
- SEO
- Conversion optimization
- Content strategy
- Performance improvement

**Copy rule:** SEO, CRO, and content strategy under GROW are capabilities Clouvent offers and develops — not a proven results track record. Use approach/methodology language ("we bring an SEO-informed approach") rather than results language ("we grow bookings by X%").

**Technology standard:** Next.js is Clouvent's default technology for future client (BUILD) projects. Treat it as the default, not an absolute rule — if a future project has a strong technical reason for a different stack, explain the trade-offs and get approval before deviating.

## 8. Competitive Differentiation

Clouvent differentiates against two adjacent categories:
1. **Cheap freelance web developers** — one-off builds, no ongoing relationship, inconsistent quality.
2. **DIY builders** (Squarespace/Showit/Wix) — generic templates, no true design partnership, no dedicated management/growth support.

Differentiation is the continuity of BUILD → MANAGE → GROW as one relationship, at a premium tier neither alternative offers.

## 9. Portfolio / Proof

Use **"Selected Work"** framing — never imply a large client portfolio.

**Do not use:** "Trusted by leading brands," "Hundreds of clients," "Our clients achieved...," or any other unsupported social proof.

Once Upon a Time Photography is currently the primary and strongest case study.

- Design and development both handled directly

## 11. Website Goals

1. Support the personalized-outreach acquisition motion — the site must read as immediately credible to a prospect who just received a direct, personalized message.
2. Convert qualified Australian photographers/visual businesses into project enquiries.
3. Present the Once Upon a Time Photography case study as credible proof of craft, within the no-fabrication rule.
4. Establish premium positioning distinct from cheap freelance/DIY alternatives.
5. Build a foundation for organic SEO/content over time (lowest current priority).

## 12. Primary Website Audience

Australian photographers and visual/creative business owners — including those arriving via personalized outbound outreach who are actively evaluating whether Clouvent is legitimate, not just those discovering it organically.

## 13. Primary Conversion Goal

Project enquiry form.

## 14. Secondary Conversion Goals

Direct email and discovery call, offered as an alternative path. No newsletter or lead magnet at launch.

## 15. Customer Journey

**Outreach-originated path:** Personalized audit/message → prospect visits site to verify legitimacy → Selected Work/case study → trust signals → service understanding → enquiry.

**Organic path (lower priority initially):** Search/referral/social → Selected Work → trust → service → enquiry.

Both paths converge on: enquiry → discovery conversation → BUILD → MANAGE → GROW.

## 16. Content Strategy

Content must be concise, confident, premium, human, specific, and conversion-oriented (per `CLAUDE.md`). All GROW-related copy is framed as capability, not proven results. All case-study copy sticks to delivered work, never business outcomes.

Drafting real page copy is blocked on: company bio, Once Upon a Time Photography's photography niche, and the testimonial.

## 17. Australian Market Strategy

Local/AU SEO principles are defined in `CLAUDE.md` (location-based intent, structured data, Google Business Profile where applicable, no artificial location pages). Pakenham, Victoria can anchor local SEO for the case study.

**[NEEDS INPUT]** Clouvent's own base location, for footer/schema.

## 18. Future International Expansion

Clouvent should feel internationally capable — international-caliber design standards and modern best practices — without claiming an existing international client base or geographic reach it doesn't have.

## 19. Lead Generation Strategy

Priority order:
1. Personalized Australian photographer website outreach
2. Referrals
3. Instagram/social presence
4. SEO/content over time

**Outreach funnel:** Find Australian photography websites → audit UX/design/performance → identify real opportunities → personalized redesign/value proposition → contact → discovery conversation → BUILD → MANAGE → GROW.

**Hard rule:** No fake audits or fabricated prospect problems — every audit must reflect genuine analysis of that business's actual site. This is a deliberate quality-over-volume strategy, not a scalable mass-outreach tactic.
